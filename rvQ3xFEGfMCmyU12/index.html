Team plan facts behind the table
  • Team monthly: 1,118 payers and $77.9k MRR (33% of all MRR; the sum of each active subscription's last charge). 826 paid the full $79 on their last charge. Of the other 292, 205 pay $39.50 and 43 upgraded mid-cycle and have not reached their first full charge yet.
  • 198 of the 826 came up from Starter or Pro on the same subscription or after paying on them (232 counting the 43 mid-cycle upgraders). How many of them use MCP is unknown.
  • Full-price moves up to Team from Starter or Pro, during or after the trial: 102 (Jun), 85 (Jul) and 64 (Aug) confirmed by a full $79 charge. Counting every move without a coupon, before its first full charge: 117, 101, 77 and 84 (Jun to Sep). September has 42 confirmed so far, with 40 more not yet due.
  • The hand-made workaround: about 13 blocked callers were offered a 3-month discount code. Of the 9 found in Stripe, 2 are on an MCP plan today (paying $15.80 and $30.09), 1 left, 1 went back to Starter, and 5 are still on Starter or Pro: 1 never received the code, 1 put off the decision, 1 chose a 50% code on another plan, and 2 accepted on the call but did not move (one of those 2 is a likely, not certain, match).
Does MCP access keep people longer?
  • Second-payment rate by the plan people started their trial on, trials from 27 Jul to 6 Sep: Team (has MCP) 49.5% (279 people), Starter 53.4% (526), Pro 61.3% (119). Trials from 8 Jun to 19 Jul: Team 49.6%, Starter 56.3%, Pro 47.8%. Team is not ahead in either window.
  • Monthly churn by price paid, Jun to Aug, failed payments included: Team 23.9%, Pro 28.8%, Starter 30.8%. Team payers churn less, but price and buyer intent are mixed in.
  • No per-customer MCP or API usage is readable in GHL, Stripe or our MCP tools.
Credits: what MCP use costs a customer, and what top-ups earn
  • Credits included each month, shared across the account: Starter 50, Pro 150, Team 400, Agency 800 (pricing page, read 28 Sep).
  • MCP charges per result or per call, multiplied by a credit rate set per plan; looking up an ad, transcribing and the swipe file are free (our MCP tool descriptions, read 2 Oct).
  • Credits per typical agent request are not settled. Our 22 Sep prompt tests summed 0.15 to 0.5 credits per prompt from the per-call meter, but the account balance fell 3.7 to 10.6 credits per run while other runs shared the account.
  • Credit packs, one-time, in Stripe: 100 credits for $20, 300 for $50, 600 for $100. Since 1 Jun: 111 bought by 70 customers, $3,287 in total, $560 to $1,140 a month. Buyers on Starter or Pro spent $40 and $60 in Aug and Sep; buyers on Team or Agency $680 and $667. 60 of the 70 bought in one month only. There is no recurring credit add-on.
  • TrendTrack puts API and MCP on its $89 Pro plan with 10,000 separate API credits a month, not on its $59 Starter (our teardown, 18 Aug).
