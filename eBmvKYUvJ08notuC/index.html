Becoming the strategist

What Vitaley needs to know before the first paid pilot

What you already have

Use from day one
  • The market view. GetHookd library plus the winner recipe: winning, active, 30+ days live, brands with 40+ ads
  • Ad production. Clone Ads and 44 MCP tools, which neither competitor has
  • What buyers want. 40+ onboarding calls with ecom owners
  • Creative frameworks. Hormozi hook, meat, CTA and the kaleidoscope; 20 ad frameworks; 5 levels of ad copy
  • Media-buying rules. Kill, scale, hold from running BMV and Capital Recreation on Meta

What you need to learn

The gap, about 4 weeks
  • Read an ecom Meta account. Six numbers: spend, ROAS or CPA against their target, CTR, hook rate, hold rate, frequency
  • Find what broke. Low hook rate means the first 3 seconds. Low hold rate means the body. Good CTR with bad sales means the offer or the page
  • Plan tests. How many new concepts vs iterations per week, and why creative is now the targeting on Meta
  • Write a brief. Angle, 3 hooks, format, the reference ad, CTA. One page per ad
  • Ecom benchmarks. Your BMV numbers are lead-gen. The logic transfers, the dollar thresholds don't
