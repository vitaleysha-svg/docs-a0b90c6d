Onuha buyers sheet to ActiveCampaign

How to make every new buyer get the welcome emails, using Zapier. For Patrick and Marius. About 15 minutes. Written 2 Oct 2026.

What you are building

1. Someone buys on onuha.com. They get access and the Whop receipt straight away.

2. Within about 5 minutes they appear as one new row in the Google Sheet "Onuha Buyers".

3. Your Zap sees the new row and adds that person to ActiveCampaign with a tag.

4. The tag starts the welcome emails.

What you needHave these open before you start.

Three rules of the sheet. Each buyer has exactly one row, for ever. Rows are never deleted: a refund changes status to removed. Staff and test accounts are left out.
Who gets what. The welcome can go to every active buyer. Anything promotional goes only to people with email_consent TRUE.

Step 1: get ActiveCampaign readyDo this first, so the Zap has something to start.

1Make the tag and the welcome automationActiveCampaign
In ActiveCampaign, create a tag called onuha-buyer. Build the welcome automation with the start trigger Tag is added: onuha-buyer, set to run once per contact. Leave the automation inactive for now.WhyThe Zap only has to add one tag. The tag starts the emails, and "once" means nobody gets the welcome twice.

Steps 2 to 11: build the ZapClick a step to fold it away when it is done.

2Start a new ZapZapier
Go to zapier.com, click Create, then Zaps.WhyOne Zap does the whole job: sheet row in, ActiveCampaign contact out.
3Trigger: Google Sheets, New Spreadsheet RowZapier
Click the Trigger box. App: Google Sheets. Trigger event: New Spreadsheet Row.WhyIt fires once for each new buyer row. Do not pick "New or Updated Spreadsheet Row" here, or a later status change would fire the welcome again.
4Connect the right Google accountZapier
Under Account, sign in with the Google account the sheet was shared with (the one that can open "Onuha Buyers").WhyZapier can only read sheets that account can open. View access is enough for a trigger.
5Pick the sheet and the tabZapier
Spreadsheet: Onuha Buyers. Worksheet: Buyers.Not in the dropdown? Choose Custom and paste the sheet ID. The ID is the long code in the sheet link, between /d/ and /edit.WhyThe Buyers tab is the list. The Sync tab only shows when the sheet last updated.
6Test the triggerZapier
Click Test trigger. You should see one buyer row with an email and a status.WhyThis proves Zapier can read the sheet before you build the rest.
7Add a filter: status is activeZapier
Click +, choose Filter by Zapier. Only continue if: status, (Text) Exactly matches, active.WhySomeone who refunded before their row landed arrives as "removed". The filter keeps them out of the welcome. Filter steps need a paid Zapier plan.
8Action: ActiveCampaign, Create or Update ContactZapier
Click +. App: ActiveCampaign. Action event: Create or Update Contact. Connect your ActiveCampaign account. Email: pick the email column from step 3. List: your Onuha list. Tags: onuha-buyer.Optional: map email_consent to a custom field, so you can keep promotions to people who said yes.Why"Create or Update" never makes a duplicate: a new person is created, an existing contact just gets the tag.
9Test with your own emailZapier
Before you click Test step, replace the Email value with your own email. Run the test, check the contact and tag appear in ActiveCampaign, then switch Email back to the email column.WhyA Zapier test writes real data. The sample row is a real buyer, so testing with their email would put a real customer through an untested setup.
10Publish, then switch the automation onZapier
Click Publish in Zapier. Then set the welcome automation to Active in ActiveCampaign.WhyPublishing first means no buyer is missed between the two clicks.
11Add the people already in the sheetActiveCampaign
Anyone in the sheet from before you published: add them to ActiveCampaign by hand and give them the onuha-buyer tag (status active only).WhyThe Zap only fires for rows added after it is published. Earlier rows never trigger it.

Later: stop emails when someone refundsBuild this second, once the welcome works.

A second Zap for removed buyers

When a buyer refunds or loses access, their row stays and status changes to removed.

  1. New Zap. Trigger: Google Sheets, New or Updated Spreadsheet Row, same sheet and tab. Set the trigger column to status if Zapier offers it.
  2. Filter: status exactly matches removed.
  3. Action: ActiveCampaign, Remove Contact From Automation, or Add/Remove Tag From Contact to add a tag like onuha-removed that ends your automations.

Until this exists, check the sheet for removed rows once a day and stop those contacts by hand.

If something does not work

The sheet is not in Zapier's dropdown

Check you connected the Google account the sheet was shared with. Then choose Custom in the Spreadsheet field and paste the sheet ID: the long code in the sheet link between /d/ and /edit.

Test trigger finds no rows

Check the Worksheet is Buyers, not Sync. Open the sheet and confirm there is at least one buyer row under the header.

Someone bought but got no welcome

Check in this order: their row is in the sheet with status active; the Zap is published; the Zap history shows a run for that row; the automation is Active in ActiveCampaign. A row that was in the sheet before you published never triggers the Zap: add that person by hand.

How fast is it?

The row lands in the sheet within about 5 minutes of the purchase. Zapier then picks it up on its own schedule, which depends on your Zapier plan. Access to the site and the Whop receipt are instant and do not depend on any of this.

Can I edit the sheet?

It is view only for you, on purpose. If you need your own notes or columns, tell Vitaliy: columns from I onwards are free, but the header row and columns A to H must not change or the sheet stops updating.
